What drives churn?

Conclusion & recommended actions

  • Biggest risk group: month-to-month, fiber-optic customers in their first 6 months who pay by electronic check.
  • Lock in contracts: offer discounts to move month-to-month customers to 1–2 year plans (churn falls from 43% to 11% and 3%).
  • Onboard early: add check-ins and offers in months 0–6, when churn is about 53%.
  • Bundle support: pair tech support and online security with fiber plans; customers without tech support churn at 42% vs 15% with it.
  • Autopay: nudge electronic-check users to automatic payment.
  • Model choice: all three models score ROC-AUC of about 0.83–0.84. Use logistic regression or random forest to catch more churners (recall about 0.79), or gradient boosting for fewer false alarms (precision 0.63).
